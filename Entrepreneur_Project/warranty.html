Return Policy

We at PC Forge are committed to providing high-quality custom-built computers that meet your specific needs and expectations. We stand behind our products and want to ensure your satisfaction with your purchase. Therefore, we offer the following warranty for your custom PC:

Your custom PC comes with a standard warranty period of 2 years from the date of purchase. During this time, we will repair or replace any defective parts or components free of charge.

Our warranty covers any defects in materials or workmanship that may arise during the warranty period. This includes any hardware issues such as motherboard failure, power supply malfunction, or defective RAM. Our warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, or neglect.

In the event that your custom PC needs repair or replacement, we will work with you to find the best solution. If a component needs repair, we will provide a replacement part at no charge. If the entire PC needs replacement, we will build and ship a new custom PC to you.

If your custom PC needs to be shipped to us for repair or replacement, we will cover the shipping costs for the return of the PC to you.

We offer technical support for the lifetime of your custom PC. If you have any issues or questions about your PC, we are available to assist you via email, phone, or online chat.

Please note that our warranty is non-transferable and is only applicable to the original purchaser of the custom PC.
