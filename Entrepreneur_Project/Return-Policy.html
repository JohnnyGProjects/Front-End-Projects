Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your custom PC purchase from our company. If for any reason you are not satisfied, you may return the item within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange, subject to the following conditions:

The custom PC must be returned in its original packaging with all included accessories.
The custom PC must not have been modified, overclocked, or damaged in any way.
Proof of purchase must be provided.
The customer is responsible for the cost of return shipping.
We reserve the right to charge a restocking fee of up to 15% of the purchase price for any returns that do not meet the above conditions.

To initiate a return, please contact our customer service team at [insert contact information]. We will provide you with a return authorization number and instructions on how to return the item.

Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it to ensure it meets the above conditions. If everything checks out, we will process your refund or exchange within 7-10 business days. Please note that refunds will be issued to the original form of payment.

If you received a damaged or defective custom PC, please contact our customer service team immediately. We will arrange for a replacement or repair as soon as possible.

Please note that we cannot accept returns or exchanges for custom PC orders that have been specially configured or built to your specifications, unless the product is defective or damaged.
