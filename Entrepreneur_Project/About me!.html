About Us

Founded in 2023 by three Carleton University students, PCForge specializes in the sale of assembled PCs and individual computer components. Our focus is on delivering high-quality hardware and dependable customer service.

We offer pre-built systems for gamers, creators, and professionals, as well as individual components such as GPUs, CPUs, motherboards, and power supplies—allowing customers to build systems tailored to their needs.

By staying current with industry trends and emerging technologies, PCForge continues to expand its offerings while providing a seamless shopping experience.

Founder

Johnny is the founder and CEO of PCForge. He is an aspiring entrepreneur with a passion for technology and business, currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in Information Technology at Carleton University in Ottawa, Ontario.
